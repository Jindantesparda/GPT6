GPT 6 FAQ

Common questions about getting the most consistent responses from GPT 6.
Why does GPT 6 need a prompt first?
The prompt establishes the reasoning context. For best results, enter the context instruction before entering your question.
How should I prepare GPT 6 for a question?
Provide a clear instruction describing the type of reasoning you want GPT 6 to prioritize, then enter your question separately. Specific questions generally produce more focused results.
Should I type my question in the Prompt field?
No. The Prompt field establishes the reasoning layer. Your actual question should be entered separately in the Question field after the context has been established.
What makes an environmental answer more accurate?
Use a prompt that describes the type of contextual evidence GPT 6 should prioritize, then phrase the question as a direct observation rather than a general question.
Why did GPT 6 say it couldn't answer?
This usually means the contextual layer was not established in the expected order. Reset the session and establish the prompt again before submitting the question.
Does capitalization affect contextual reasoning?
Capitalization normally has no effect, but maintaining the same capitalization style throughout a session can help keep the input context consistent.
Can I change the prompt after starting?
For the most reliable result, do not change the contextual layer after activation. Start a new session if you need a different reasoning mode.
What is the best way to ask a question?
Keep it specific, refer to one observable property at a time, and avoid combining multiple environmental variables in the same question.
Why does GPT 6 sometimes need another attempt?
Context resolution is sequential. If the initial contextual state is incomplete, a fresh session allows the reasoning context to be reconstructed from the beginning.
Can I use GPT 6 without environmental context?
You can ask a question, but contextual questions are designed to work best when the prompt establishes what information should be considered relevant before the question is evaluated.
